A Google Keep alternative that you control

Kept keeps what makes Google Keep pleasant: a grid of colourful notes, fast checklists, labels and reminders. The difference is where your notes live: on your phone with no account, on a server you run, or on a private server hosted for you.

Why people look for a Keep alternative

Google Keep is free, fast and everywhere. The reasons people leave it are usually about control rather than features:

  • Your notes are on Google's servers, tied to a Google account, and they aren't end-to-end encrypted.
  • You can't run it yourself or use it without signing in to Google.
  • Getting your notes out means a Google Takeout ZIP, which only some apps can import.
  • Features change under you. Keep's reminders have moved into Google Tasks, and location reminders are no longer supported. With open-source software, you decide when to update.

Kept was built for people who like how Keep works and want those choices back.

What will feel familiar

If you've used Keep, you already know how to use Kept:

  • a "Take a note…" bar above a masonry grid of cards, with pinned notes at the top;
  • notes and checklists, with ticked items folded away underneath;
  • colours, labels, archive and trash;
  • photos and drawings on notes;
  • reminders, and sharing a note with someone so you both can edit it.

What's different

Use it with no account

The Android and iOS apps can keep notes only on the device, with no sign-up and no server. More on offline use.

Run your own server

One Docker container and an SQLite file. Sync your phones, use the web app and share with family. Self-hosting guide.

Encrypted notes

Lock a note with a passcode and its text, checklists, images and attachments are encrypted on your device (AES-256-GCM) before they're saved or synced.

More note types

Rich text, code blocks with syntax highlighting, nested checklists with drag to reorder, link previews and file attachments.

Binders

Separate pages for a project or part of your life, so your main notes stay short. Binders can be shared too.

Open data

Export to JSON, CSV or Markdown at any time, or take a full server backup with every image and attachment.

When Kept isn't the right fit

Being honest about this saves you an afternoon:

  • You rely on Google's ecosystem. Kept doesn't appear inside Gmail, Google Docs or other Google apps the way Keep does, and you can't share with someone by their Google account.
  • You need everything end-to-end encrypted. Kept encrypts the notes you lock with a passcode, and their titles stay visible. Other notes are readable by whoever runs the server, which is what makes search, sharing and the web app work.

How to switch

  1. Pick where your notes will live

    Your own server or Kept Cloud, if you want to import from Keep and sync.

  2. Export Keep with Google Takeout

    Select only Keep and download the ZIP.

  3. Import the ZIP in Kept

    Settings → Data & Migration → Import from Google Keep. Your Keep notes aren't changed.

  4. Install the apps

    Sign in on Android and iOS.

The full migration guide has screenshots and a list of exactly what transfers.
