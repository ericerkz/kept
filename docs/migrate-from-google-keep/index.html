Kept › Migrate from Google Keep

Export your Google Keep notes and move them to Kept

Google Takeout gives you a ZIP of everything in Keep. Kept reads that ZIP directly, so your checklists, labels, colours, pins, images and dates come across in one step, without copying notes by hand.

Applies to Kept 1.9.8 · Last checked November 2026

Short version: on takeout.google.com, deselect everything except Keep and create an export. Then in Kept, open Settings → Data & Migration → Import from Google Keep and choose the ZIP. Your Google Keep notes are copied, not moved, so nothing changes in Keep.

Before you start

The importer runs on a Kept server, so you need one of these:

Your own server

Install Kept with Docker in a few minutes. See the self-hosting guide.

Kept Cloud

A private Kept server that's hosted and backed up for you. Start a free trial.

The import tool isn't available in the mobile apps' device-only mode. If you only want notes on your phone, import into a server first, then sign in to it from the app. The app keeps a full copy for offline use.

Setting up a new server? On the setup screen, tick Import from Google Keep and Kept takes you straight to the importer after you create your account.

Step 1: export Keep with Google Takeout

  1. Open Google Takeout

    Go to takeout.google.com and sign in with the Google account that has your notes.

  2. Select only Keep

    Click Deselect all, scroll down the list and tick Keep. Exporting other services makes the file much larger and slower to import.

  3. Create the export

    Click Next step. Leave the file type as .zip, choose to receive a download link by email, and click Create export.

  4. Download the ZIP

    Google emails you a link when the export is ready, usually within minutes, but it can take longer for big libraries. The link expires after about a week. Download the ZIP and keep it as it is; there's no need to unzip it.

Inside the ZIP, each note is in Takeout/Keep/ as a .json file (which Kept reads) and an .html file (for reading in a browser), alongside any images. Google lists what the export includes in Export your data from Google Keep.

Step 2: import the ZIP into Kept

  1. Open the importer

    In the Kept web app, open Settings and scroll to Data & Migration. The same steps are repeated under How to get your Google Takeout export.

  2. Choose the ZIP

    Click Choose Takeout ZIP and pick the file you downloaded. Large exports take a while to upload; keep the tab open until the result appears.

  3. Check the summary

    Kept reports how many notes it imported and pinned, and how many it skipped, such as notes that were in Keep's trash.

What transfers, and what doesn't

This is what Kept's importer does with each part of a Google Keep note, based on the importer's code in Kept 1.9.8:

From Google KeepIn Kept
Titles and textImported, with line breaks.
ChecklistsImported, with ticked items and indented sub-items.
LabelsImported. Labels are created as needed and matched to existing ones by name.
ColoursImported, mapped to Kept's matching colours.
Pinned and archived notesImported as pinned or archived.
Images and drawingsImported when Takeout includes them as PNG, JPEG, GIF or WebP files.
Links saved on a noteImported as links at the end of the note.
Created and edited datesKept, so notes stay in their original order.
Text formatting (bold, headings)Plain text. The importer reads Keep's plain-text version of each note.
Notes in Keep's trashSkipped.
Audio recordings and other filesNot imported. Only images are brought across.
RemindersNot in the export. Google Takeout doesn't list reminders for Keep, which now stores them in Google Tasks. Set them again in Kept, which supports time and repeating reminders, plus location reminders in the native apps.
CollaboratorsNot imported. They're Google accounts, so share the note again with people on your Kept server.

Running the import twice is safe: notes that were already imported (same creation date, title and text) are skipped, so you can import a newer Takeout export to pick up recent notes.

After the import

  • Get the apps. Install Kept Notes from the App Store or Google Play and sign in to the same server. Your imported notes sync down and work offline.
  • Re-create reminders on the notes that need them.
  • Lock sensitive notes. Locking a note with a passcode encrypts its contents on your device before it's saved.
  • Tidy up with binders. Binders are optional separate pages for a project or area of life, which keeps your main notes view short.
  • Keep Google Keep until you're happy. The import doesn't change anything in Google Keep. Delete your Keep notes only when you're sure everything you need is in Kept.

Troubleshooting

"No Google Keep notes found in this ZIP"

Kept looks for Takeout/Keep/*.json inside the ZIP. Upload the original Takeout ZIP rather than a re-zipped folder, and check that Keep was ticked in Takeout. If Takeout split a big export into several ZIPs, import each one.

The upload fails or stops part way

Large Takeout files are often blocked by a reverse proxy or CDN (Cloudflare, Nginx) before they reach Kept. Raise your proxy's upload limit, or import over a direct LAN or VPN connection to the server. Kept accepts Takeout ZIPs up to 5 GB by default; self-hosters can raise that with KEPT_TAKEOUT_UPLOAD_MAX.

Some notes say "could not be imported"

Those notes' JSON files couldn't be read. Everything else is still imported. Opening the matching .html file from the ZIP shows you the note so you can copy it across by hand.

Can I import into the phone app without a server?

Not at the moment. Import into a server (your own or Kept Cloud), then sign in to it from the app.

Leaving Kept later

You shouldn't get locked in again. From Settings → Data & Migration you can export your notes as JSON, CSV or Markdown at any time. Those exports cover notes, checklists and labels, not images. To move a whole server, including images and attachments, use the full server backup described in the self-hosting guide.
