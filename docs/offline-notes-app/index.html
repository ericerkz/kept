Two ways to be offline

"Offline" means two different things for a notes app, and Kept does both:

Device-only

No account, no server, no network. Notes are stored only on this phone or tablet. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Offline-first sync

Connected to Kept Cloud or your own server, the app still keeps a full copy on the device. You can read and edit with no signal, and changes sync when you're back online.

Start with no account

  1. Install Kept Notes

    From Google Play or the App Store. Both are an optional one-time purchase that helps support ongoing development, with no subscription and no ads. Kept is open source (AGPL-3.0).

  2. Choose "Use on this device"

    It's on the welcome screen. You won't be asked to sign up or sign in.

  3. Start writing

    That's it. You can switch to a server later from Settings.

What works on the device

Device-only mode isn't a cut-down version. It keeps notes, checklists, images, drawings, attachments, labels and reminders on the device, and the phone integrations work too:

Notes and checklists

Rich text, code blocks, nested checklists with drag to reorder, colours, background patterns, pins, labels and binders.

Photos, drawings and files

Add photos, sketch on a canvas, or attach files to a note.

Reminders

One-off and repeating reminders, plus location reminders that trigger when you arrive at or leave a place. On iOS they appear in Apple Reminders.

Widgets

Home screen widgets on Android and iOS for browsing notes and quick capture. On iOS you can tick checklist items from the widget.

Share into Kept

Share text, links, photos and files from other apps and Kept saves them as a note.

Smart Capture

On supported phones, describe a note or reminder in your own words and Kept creates it using an on-device model.

Back up a device-only library

When notes only exist on your phone, losing the phone or deleting the app can mean losing them. Kept makes backups explicit:

  • Settings → Notes on this device → Save a complete backup saves one file with your notes, images, attachments, reminders and preferences. Save it somewhere outside the app, such as your computer or a cloud drive you trust.
  • Restore a backup, in the same place, brings that library back on an iPhone, iPad or Android device.
  • Optional backup reminders prompt you now and then. Backups are only saved when you choose to create them.

Add sync later

If you start on one device and later want your notes on a second phone, a tablet or the web, go to Settings → Notes on this device → Move my notes to a server. Choose Kept Cloud or your own server. Your whole library moves across, including images and attachments.

From then on the app works offline-first: it keeps a local copy, saves edits while you have no connection, and syncs them when the server is reachable. The web app also keeps a local copy and holds changes if your connection drops, but for dependable offline use on the go, use the native apps.

What needs a server

These features need a Kept server, because they involve another device, another person or another service:

  • sync between devices, and the web app;
  • sharing notes and binders, and live collaboration;
  • importing a Google Keep Takeout export (see migrating from Keep);
  • calendar sync (Google Calendar or CalDAV), OAuth and MCP integrations.

Coming from Google Keep? Keep needs a Google account and stores everything on Google's servers. Kept's device-only mode is the opposite: nothing leaves your phone unless you decide to connect a server. See Kept as a Google Keep alternative.

Common questions

Does Kept work in airplane mode?

Yes. In device-only mode it never needs a connection. In connected mode you can keep reading and editing, and changes sync when you're back online.

Do I need to create an account?

Not for device-only mode. An account is only needed on a server, which you'd run yourself or get through Kept Cloud.

Can I move a device-only library from iPhone to Android?

Yes. Save a complete backup on one device and restore it on the other with Restore a backup.

Where exactly are my notes stored?

In device-only mode, in the app's storage on your phone. In connected mode, on the server you chose, with a copy on each device for offline use. See the privacy policy for details.
