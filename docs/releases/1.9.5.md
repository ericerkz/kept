# Kept 1.9.5

## Features

- **Shared binders:** Share a whole binder, including notes added later. Members can contribute their own notes, edit together, remove notes from the binder, and move notes to Trash. Thanks to **@B85S5DSG** for suggesting this in [#137](https://github.com/ericerkz/kept/issues/137)!
- **Kept is ready for community translations! 🌍** You can now help translate Kept’s web interface and native Android/iOS screens together through one public catalogue. Partial translations are welcome—you don’t need to translate everything to contribute! English is currently the only included language. See the [translation guide](https://github.com/ericerkz/kept/blob/master/translations/README.md) to get started. Thanks to **@SvenPausH**, **@Osvaldo-Net**, and **@zombie241088** for requesting this in [#15](https://github.com/ericerkz/kept/issues/15) and [#115](https://github.com/ericerkz/kept/issues/115)!
- **iOS widgets are here! 🎉** Keep your notes front and centre on your Home Screen. Choose recent or pinned notes, a label or binder, or give a single note its own widget. Tick off checklist items directly from the widget, or jump straight into creating a note, checklist, drawing, or photo note.
- **Expanded note colours and a revised picker:** Choose from 54 colours, organised by colour family, with separate **Colour / Background** tabs and a compact layout that follows the note.

## Fixes

- Fixed multiple links becoming joined together or disappearing behind rich previews after closing and reopening a note. Thanks to **@B85S5DSG** for reporting [#136](https://github.com/ericerkz/kept/issues/136)!
- Fixed tablet layouts where the new-note bar could sit behind the navbar, and remembered the sidebar’s expanded/collapsed state. Thanks to **@bluetigger68** for [#140](https://github.com/ericerkz/kept/issues/140)!
- Improved portrait iPad layouts to show three note columns when there’s enough room.
- Smoothed out mobile sidebar closing and corrected Android Back handling during the transition. Thanks to **@amrear** for [#131](https://github.com/ericerkz/kept/pull/131)!
- Made mobile editor and sidebar icons consistent in size and colour. Thanks to **@amrear** for [#132](https://github.com/ericerkz/kept/pull/132) and [#134](https://github.com/ericerkz/kept/pull/134)!
- Removed the solid background around the browser favicon. Thanks to **@amrear** for [#133](https://github.com/ericerkz/kept/pull/133)!
- Tidied the binder and label editors, removing redundant reorder arrows, showing collaborator avatars for shared binders, and adding note counts for labels.
- Added image thumbnails to device-only widgets on Android and iOS.
- Stopped mobile keyboards automatically capitalising usernames during sign-in, registration, and initial setup.
- Fixed long-pressing pinned notes selecting the **Other** heading on mobile.
- Added a message explaining that reminders are paused while a note is archived. Thanks to **@GitToSpace** for raising this in [#130](https://github.com/ericerkz/kept/issues/130)!
- Improved the iOS client-certificate import UI, making the password field clearer and easier to tap, with more separation from the Import button. Thanks to **@DualTachyon** for the feedback in [#82](https://github.com/ericerkz/kept/issues/82)!

*Mobile improvements require the corresponding Android or iOS app update.*
